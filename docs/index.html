Discover Our Turkish Heritage

Welcome to Istanbul Restaurant, your gateway to the rich and flavourful world of Turkish cuisine. Located in the heart of Crewe, our restaurant brings the warmth, hospitality, and time-honoured recipes of Turkey straight to your table.

We specialise in authentic Turkish dishes prepared with traditional methods and the freshest ingredients—from sizzling kebabs and hearty stews to fragrant rice, mezes, and fresh-baked pide. Each plate tells a story of centuries-old culinary heritage.

Whether you're looking for a cosy spot for family dinner, a romantic evening, or a cultural food experience, Istanbul Restaurant offers a warm atmosphere and genuine flavours that feel like home.

Come and taste Turkey on a plate —your journey starts here.

Turkish Breakfast

Start your day the Turkish way with our authentic Turkish breakfast (kahvaltı) —a lavish spread of olives, cheeses, fresh tomatoes, cucumbers, eggs, jams, simit, and warm bread served with strong Turkish tea. It’s not just a meal—it’s a ritual.

Sizzling Kebabs

Indulge in our perfectly seasoned kebabs, grilled to perfection and served with fragrant rice and vegetables.

Fresh-Baked Pide

Try our traditional pide, a delicious Turkish flatbread baked fresh and topped with your choice of ingredients.
